A short game about a guy who has to escape a carnival by answering riddles.

# Storyline:
It starts with the user seeing that he has been fired from his company. What he also notices is the little button on the right-corner of the pop up asking "_Want to Escape?_"
When he clicks it he is taken into a world of joy and happiness -- a **Carnival**. There he haves fun, laughing all the while and smiling on the rides. But, as he decides to 
leave the place he is trapped by a cunning joker who askes him to answer his **riddles** to escape.

<img width="945" height="440" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/b4d72321-bac7-4171-9ea5-217197274670" />


You have 3 lives and there are **9 riddles** and for each right answer you a get **a piece** of a **puzzle**. If you get all the riddles right then you get to solve the puzzle
with all the pieces.
But, if you lose all your lives........._(try it yourself!!)_

# Game Mechanics
You have three lives

There are 9 riddles

Each question has four options out of which only one is correct

For each correct answer you get a piece of the puzzle

For each wrong answer you lose a life



# Tech Stack
Unlike traditional games, this game uses _html_, _css_ for styling, and _js_ for backend.
